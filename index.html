變形金剛：台灣大富翁 遊戲規則

1. 遊戲背景與目標

賽博坦星的能量晶體散落在寶島台灣！博派與狂派金剛登陸台灣各地，爭奪土地控制權並建造能量站。讓所有其他對手金剛破產的玩家將成為最後贏家！

2. 角色與特殊能力

  • 柯博文 (Optimus Prime)：博派領袖，受【領導母體】庇佑，起始額外獲得 $500 能量儲備。
  • 大黃蜂 (Bumblebee)：偵查先鋒，啟動【超速偵查】，若擲出雙骰額外獎勵 $200 巡航補貼。
  • 密卡登 (Megatron)：狂派帝王，憑藉【霸王威壓】，購買地產價格享 10% 折扣優惠。
  • 天王星 (Starscream)：空戰霸主，具備【超音速飛行】，停在他人無建築地產上有 20% 機率匿蹤免收過路費。
  • 鐵皮 (Ironhide)：博派重裝，發動【重裝裝甲】，繳納稅金減免 20%。
  • 聲波 (Soundwave)：狂派情報，啟動【能量監控網絡】，公共事業過路費收益提升 50%。

3. 地產收購與壟斷升級

  • 普通地產：購買後他人停留在該處需支付過路費。當集齊同色系全部地產（達成壟斷）後，過路費翻倍，並可在自己回合建造【能量站】（最多4座）及終極【賽博坦要塞】！
  • 台灣高鐵站 (4座)：擁有的高鐵站越多，過路費成倍暴增 ($250 → $500 → $1,000 → $2,000)。
  • 公共事業 (台電能量網、水庫)：過路費依據對手擲出的骰子點數乘上倍數。

4. 特殊格子與卡片

  • 起點 (GO)：每次經過或停留可領取 $2,000 能量金。
  • 機會 (Chance) & 命運 (Fate)：抽取具備台灣特色與變形金剛故事的隨機卡片（如太空橋傳送、台積電分紅、空襲演習、美食節獎金等）。
  • 維修基地 / 拘留所：進入後需支付 $500 保釋金、使用特赦卡或連擲出雙骰方能脫困。

1. Background & Objective

Cybertronian Energon crystals have scattered across Taiwan! Autobots and Decepticons deploy across the island to seize territory and construct fortified bases. The last Transformer standing after bankrupting all rivals rules Taiwan!

2. Transformers Roster & Unique Abilities

  • Optimus Prime (柯博文): Autobots Leader. Blessed by the 【Matrix of Leadership】, starts with an extra +$500 Energon reserve.
  • Bumblebee (大黃蜂): Scout Vanguard. Uses 【Speed Scout】 to earn +$200 bonus speed allowance whenever rolling Doubles.
  • Megatron (密卡登): Decepticons Emperor. Applies 【Tyrant Dominance】 to receive a 10% discount on all unowned property purchases.
  • Starscream (天王星): Air Commander. Equipped with 【Supersonic Stealth】, having a 20% chance to evade rent on unbuilt properties.
  • Ironhide (鐵皮): Weapons Specialist. Employs 【Heavy Armor】 to reduce all tax payments by 20%.
  • Soundwave (聲波): Communications Officer. Uses 【Network Surveillance】 to boost public utility revenues by 50%.

3. Property Monopolies & Base Development

  • Street Properties: Collect rent when rivals land on your territory. Once you own all properties in a color group (Monopoly), base rent doubles, and you may build up to 4 【Energon Pods】 and a final 【Cybertron Fortress】!
  • Taiwan High Speed Rail (4 Stations): Rent escalates rapidly as you acquire more stations ($250 → $500 → $1,000 → $2,000).
  • Utilities (Taipower & Reservoir): Rent is calculated by multiplying the rival's dice roll by 40x or 100x.

4. Special Spaces & Event Cards

  • START (GO): Collect $2,000 Energon every time you pass or land on Start.
  • Chance & Fate Cards: Draw dynamic cards featuring Taiwan landmarks and Cybertron lore (Space Bridge teleportation, TSMC dividend, air raid drill, food festivals, etc.).
  • Detention / Repair Bay (Jail): Pay $500 bail, use a Pardon Card, or roll Doubles to break free!
